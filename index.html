Got questions? We got you

💕

How does the group coaching container work?

It's a monthly subscription for your body, mind and soul. Every week you get 3 workout sessions plus a yoga session, along with monthly challenges, tons of resources, and live monthly yoga sessions on Zoom. You also get full access to the community page, with active engagement, a message board, and support from all the other women in The Collective.

What's the cancellation policy?

The Collective membership is month to month with a 30-day notice cancellation policy.

Do I need gym experience?

No. Workouts start where you are, and every move comes with coaching cues. Beginners are welcome.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Every program has a home version and a gym version, and you can switch between them whenever you like.
